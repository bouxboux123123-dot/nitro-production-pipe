You can spark organic casual conversations across remote departments effortlessly without booking extra calls. Remote silos rapidly emerge when marketing, sales, and engineering never cross paths outside formal status updates. Cross-functional collaboration breaks down when employees view other departments merely as faceless Slack avatars. An automated trivia engine pairs cross-departmental peers into friendly three-person trivia squads inside Slack threads each Tuesday. This dissolves company silos and builds genuine cross-team camaraderie.
