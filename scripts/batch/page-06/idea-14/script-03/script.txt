Give your remote employees a two-minute mental break and a shared laugh between intense sprints. Staring at ticket queues and Figma files all day without casual coworker banter accelerates burnout. Remote isolation creeps in when internal communication consists strictly of project deadlines and bug escalations. Launching Donut or Kahoot async challenges in Microsoft Teams gives employees quick brain teasers and team leaderboards. This re-energizes team morale in minutes.
