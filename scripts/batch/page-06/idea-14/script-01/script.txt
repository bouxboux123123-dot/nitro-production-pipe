Here is how remote teams build real personal connections without cringey, forced Zoom games. Mandating virtual happy hours on Friday evenings breeds resentment among distributed workers wanting to unplug. Forcing introverts into awkward video icebreakers kills genuine team bonding and creates silent, disengaged meeting rooms. Instead, install Ricotta or Trivia directly into your general Slack channels for automated daily async quizzes. Colleagues banter naturally on their own schedule.
