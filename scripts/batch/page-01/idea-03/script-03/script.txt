Never leave company credentials active inside a past client's ad accounts. When retainers end, agency team logins often linger inside client Meta and Google dashboards for months. Orphaned user accounts expose your agency to severe liability if the client suffers a breach or unapproved ad spend. Automate credential revocation through 1Password and Leadsie the moment a contract termination date is logged in Airtable. This locks down enterprise security and protects your reputation.
