The absolute simplest way to build a personal daily planner that actually works. Juggling sticky notes and phone reminders leaves your important deadlines scattered everywhere. Complex productivity apps require hours of setup, causing beginners to quit after three days. Open a blank Notion page, type forward slash to add checkboxes, and group tasks by morning and afternoon. You get an uncluttered daily planner that keeps you focused, so follow for more daily workflow breakdowns.
