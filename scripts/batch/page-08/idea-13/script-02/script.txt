Stop right-clicking your mouse to copy, paste, undo, and reopen closed tabs. Hunting through drop-down context menus for everyday actions completely kills your typing momentum. Every right-click pulls your attention away from your actual document and strains your wrist. Instead, use Control-C to copy, Control-V to paste, and Control-Shift-T to resurrect accidentally closed tabs instantly. These basic keystrokes save hours of pointing and clicking.
