You can navigate your computer at lightning speed without ever touching your trackpad. Lifting your hands off the keyboard every thirty seconds creates constant micro-stalls during deep work. Relying entirely on mouse pointers slows down tab hopping, text selection, and window switching dramatically. Press Control-T for new tabs, Alt-Tab to jump applications, and Windows-V to retrieve copied items. You will keep your hands locked in typing flow, so follow for daily beginner productivity tricks.
