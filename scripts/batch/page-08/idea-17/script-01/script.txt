Did your coworker give you a spreadsheet with dates formatted in five different ways? You sit there manually retyping slashes, fixing reversed months, and editing messy cells row by row. Unformatted text dates block Google Sheets from sorting chronologically, completely breaking your filters and formulas. Instead, select your column, click Format, choose Date, and let built-in Smart Fill standardize everything instantly. This cleans hundreds of messy entries in five seconds.
