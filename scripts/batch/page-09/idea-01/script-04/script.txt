Why productivity geeks are abandoning Notion for Obsidian in 2026. Notion was built for collaborative team documentation, but managing daily tasks solo feels like running heavy enterprise software. Constant server pings and feature bloat drain laptop battery and create unnecessary typing latency. Obsidian strips away cloud bloat, indexing your personal vault locally with blazing community plugins like Dataview and Omnisearch. Switching cuts your retrieval time in half while preserving your privacy.
