Notion or Obsidian: which one should you actually use to organize your life? Notion offers sleek relational databases, but lagging web load times disrupt your daily journaling habit. Relying on proprietary cloud sync leaves your critical notes vulnerable to sudden outages. Obsidian fixes this by storing plain Markdown files locally with instant offline search and graph visualization. Pick Obsidian for private speed or Notion for collaborative workspaces, so follow for daily workflow tool breakdowns.
