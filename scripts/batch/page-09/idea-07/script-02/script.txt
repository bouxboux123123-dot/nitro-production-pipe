When to use Canva's rapid templates versus Figma's pixel-perfect vector controls. Marketers burn hours building slide decks in Figma when they need presentations ready in twenty minutes. Figma offers precise design systems and developer handoffs, but its steep learning curve stalls rapid content creation. Deploy Canva's shared templates for marketing collateral while reserving Figma for UI wireframes and app prototypes. This system triples your creative output without designer burnout.
