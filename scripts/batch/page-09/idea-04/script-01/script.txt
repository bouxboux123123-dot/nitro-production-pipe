Loom or Tella: which screen recorder makes your product walkthroughs look elite? Loom is perfect for quick internal updates, but its static camera bubble looks unpolished in client-facing sales demos. Unedited screen recordings with awkward pauses and tiny text reduce viewer retention on high-ticket pitches. Tella transforms basic recordings into cinematic videos with automatic zoom, custom backgrounds, and multi-clip layouts. This turns simple product tours into high-converting assets, so follow to scale your client acquisition engine.
