Midjourney versus Flux: which AI model creates more realistic assets for commercial brands? Midjourney produces gorgeous artistic images, but its overly stylized skin tones can ruin photorealistic product mockups. Prompting through Discord channels also scrambles team workflows and blocks direct integration into custom software pipelines. Flux provides natural textures, superior prompt adherence, and open weights you can run through ComfyUI. You get studio-grade marketing assets with total workflow control.
