Why is open-source Flux suddenly challenging Midjourney's dominance in generative AI art? Midjourney seemed untouchable, but keeping image generation behind proprietary servers created strict commercial limitations. Design teams hit walls whenever Discord queues throttled renders or shifted character consistency between campaign assets. Flux offers twelve-billion-parameter open weights, enabling custom LoRA fine-tuning and local GPU batch generation. You own your entire production pipeline without monthly caps.
