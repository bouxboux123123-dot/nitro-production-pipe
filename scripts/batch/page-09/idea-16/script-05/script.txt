Stripe versus Lemon Squeezy: here is the crucial decision every digital seller must make before launch. Picking the wrong payment structure traps you in either manual tax filings or excessive transaction cuts. Without an in-house accounting team to manage global VAT registrations, Stripe's lower processing fee quickly backfires. Launch on Lemon Squeezy to outsource compliance immediately, migrating to Stripe once you hit enterprise scale. That balance protects your runway early.
