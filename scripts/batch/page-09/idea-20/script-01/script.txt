Why are tech founders leaving Calendly to host bookings on open-source Cal.com? Calendly works for basic scheduling, but growing startups hit rigid per-seat paywalls and branding restrictions. Juggling multiple organizations and custom webhook triggers becomes expensive and fragile on closed proprietary tiers. Cal.com offers self-hosting, routing forms, and open API webhooks that sync bookings with your CRM. You gain enterprise scheduling control with zero per-seat fees.
